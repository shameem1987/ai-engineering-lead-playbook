# AI-native team operating model

**Roles**
- *AI champion* (per team): shares practices, collects feedback, triages tool issues
- *Tech lead*: owns quality bar for AI-assisted work
- *Engineering manager*: owns adoption, measurement and policy compliance

**Rituals**
- Fortnightly 30-min "what worked / what burned us" share-out
- Monthly metrics review (cycle time, defects, rework, sentiment)
- Quarterly policy and tooling review

**Definition of done (AI-assisted work)**
Understood by the author, reviewed by a human, tests behaviour not implementation, no sensitive data shared, labelled.
