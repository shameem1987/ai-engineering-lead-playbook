# Team AI-readiness checklist

Score each line 0 (no), 1 (partly), 2 (yes). A total below 60% suggests fixing foundations before scaling AI use.

## Engineering foundations
- [ ] CI runs on every PR; main is always releasable
- [ ] Meaningful automated test coverage on core paths
- [ ] Code review culture with clear standards
- [ ] Observability: logs, metrics, alerts that people trust

## Governance
- [ ] Approved-tools list and a data-handling policy exist
- [ ] Secrets scanning is enabled in repos
- [ ] Security and legal have reviewed AI vendors' terms
- [ ] An owner is named for AI tooling decisions

## People
- [ ] Engineers know what may and may not be shared with AI tools
- [ ] Training and shared prompt practices exist
- [ ] Safe space to report AI mistakes and near-misses

## Measurement
- [ ] Baseline delivery and quality metrics captured
- [ ] A way to detect rework and regressions
