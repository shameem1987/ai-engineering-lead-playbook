# 90-day plan for an engineering leader introducing AI-assisted delivery

| Days | Focus | Outputs |
|---|---|---|
| 1-30 | Listen and baseline | Metrics baseline, tool/risk inventory, stakeholder map, quick wins list |
| 31-60 | Pilot | Charter, 1-2 pilot teams, policy v1, weekly learnings |
| 61-90 | Standardize | Approved tools, prompt library, training, dashboard, roadmap for scaling |

**Review questions at day 90:** Did cycle time improve without hurting quality? What rework did AI create? What does the team want more or less of? What risk did we discover?
